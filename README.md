# The Big Picture

## Overview

**The Big Picture** is a data-driven software solution designed to help food pantries identify and respond to localized food insecurity.

Many organizations currently rely on large ZIP codes to plan food distribution. While useful for broad planning, ZIP codes can overlook smaller, low-income areas where transportation access is limited. As a result, communities experiencing food insecurity may be missed when organizations plan delivery routes and allocate resources.

The Big Picture serves as a **digital bridge between existing pantry data and hyper-local community needs**. The software works alongside existing spreadsheets and data systems to analyze neighborhood-level information and build a predictive inventory model. By analyzing data at the **11-digit Census Block level**, the system can help organizations identify areas most likely to experience shortages and proactively direct resources where they are needed most.

## Why It Matters

Traditional geographic data can make it difficult to identify small pockets of food insecurity. Using hyper-local neighborhood data allows food pantries to make more informed decisions about inventory, transportation, and distribution.

The Big Picture helps organizations:

* Anticipate potential food shortages before they occur
* Identify transportation and accessibility gaps
* Create targeted donor requests based on current and predicted needs
* Encourage resource sharing between neighboring food pantries
* Automate weight-based state compliance reporting
* Improve community impact without requiring major changes to existing workflows

## Key Features

### Predictive Inventory Modeling

Forecasts **when and where local food supplies may run out**, allowing pantries to prepare for shortages in advance.

### 11-Digit Census Block Analytics

Uses highly localized geographic data to identify **micro-neighborhood transportation and food-access gaps**.

### Background Integration

Works alongside existing **spreadsheets and data systems**, minimizing disruption to current pantry workflows.

### Smart Donor Needs Lists

Generates itemized donor requests based on **current inventory and forecasted shortages**, helping donors provide the resources communities need most.

### Sister Pantry Cooperation

Supports collaboration and resource sharing between local food pantries to improve overall distribution efficiency.

### Automated Compliance Reporting

Automatically converts inventory data into **weight-based state compliance reports**, reducing manual administrative work.

## Future Goals

Our long-term goal is to expand **The Big Picture** into an open, easy-to-use toolkit for food networks across the United States.

Future development will focus on:

* Reducing waste through more efficient distribution
* Enabling faster and more reliable state reporting
* Improving collaboration between food pantries
* Expanding access to hyper-local food insecurity data
* Helping ensure that every community can reliably access available food resources

## Vision

The Big Picture aims to close the logistical gaps that can prevent available food from reaching the communities that need it most.

By combining **predictive analytics, hyper-local geographic data, and existing pantry information**, the project seeks to make food distribution more proactive, efficient, and equitable.
